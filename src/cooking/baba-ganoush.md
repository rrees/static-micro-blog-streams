# Baba ganoush

Bake aubergine on the barbecue until the aubergine has collapsed and steamed in its skin. Scrape the flesh from the skin.

Place in a blender and add ground cumin, aleppo flakes, salt, garlic puree, tahini, olive oil and lemon juice.

Blend until smooth and then adjust seasonings according to taste.
